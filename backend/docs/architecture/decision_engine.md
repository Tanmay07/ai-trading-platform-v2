# Investment Decision Intelligence Engine (IDIE) Architecture

## Overview
The Investment Decision Intelligence Engine is the central brain for actionable portfolio advice in the AI Trading Platform. It replaces simplistic rule engines and black-box ML models with a transparent, highly configurable, and explainable expert system.

## The Four Pillars of Intelligence
Every decision generated by the engine evaluates a holding across four distinct dimensions:
1. **Technical Intelligence (30%)**: Momentum, Trend, RSI, Moving Averages.
2. **Valuation Intelligence (30%)**: Fundamental strength (PE, PB, EPS Growth).
3. **Portfolio Context (20%)**: Evaluates single-stock concentration risk and dynamically downgrades ratings if allocation limits are breached.
4. **Risk Intelligence (20%)**: Evaluates historical volatility, Max Drawdown, and Beta.

## Output Generation
The engine guarantees that a stock receives exactly one of the explicitly defined `DecisionCategory` values (e.g. `STRONG_BUY`, `BUY_MORE`, `HOLD`, `SELL`). 

Furthermore, a comprehensive `RecommendationObject` is constructed containing:
- Target Price 1 & 2
- Stop Loss
- Expected CAGR
- An exhaustive, dynamically generated `explanation_why` string that cites specific triggers from the Intelligence modules.

## Integrations
- **MarketDataService (Phase P1)**: Feeds intraday/historical candles into the Technical Engine.
- **PortfolioService (Phase P2)**: Feeds live position weights into the Portfolio Context Engine.
